How we calculated this

The process rating scores nine parameters and weights them to 100. Two come from your numbers: approval cycle time against a 3-day best-in-class benchmark (18), and suppliers managed per team member against a 15-per-person well-managed benchmark and a 60-per-person unmanageable-without-tools benchmark (10). Seven come from how you buy: price discovery method (16), competitive quotes per requirement (14), where the process is recorded (14), rush and off-contract buying (10), top-3 supplier concentration (6), how freight is bought (6), and quality checks at receipt (6). Skipped questions are dropped and the remaining weights are rescaled, so the rating always reads out of 100.

The savings range starts from Trafasa's benchmark leakage rate for Indian steel, ferro-alloys, mining and chemicals procurement — value lost to spend fragmentation, maverick buying and weak price visibility — and then scales it to your rating, so a tighter process shows a smaller leak than a loose one. Hours freed assumes automation removes recurring manual admin time per team member. These are directional estimates to start a conversation, not a guarantee — a real number needs a look at your actual spend data.
